# Code of conduct

Showpilot follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, welcome newcomers, criticise ideas rather than people, and assume good intent.
Harassment, insults, and sharing other people's private information are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/vijaykumargowdakk).
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments or block people who break this code.
